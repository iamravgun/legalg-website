Header & footer demo

This page intentionally has nothing else on it. Use it to test the mega menu (hover, click, and keyboard-only), the mobile drawer below 1024px, and the footer link columns — all built entirely from data/navigation.js.

Try: Tab to a category trigger, Enter to open it, ArrowDown into the group rail, ArrowUp/ArrowDown to move between groups, ArrowRight into the service list, Escape to close.
